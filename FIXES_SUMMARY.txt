CONFIDENCE SCORES EXPLAINED

company_confidence: How confident AI is company name is correct
  1.0 = "At [CompanyName]" (explicit mention)
  0.7 = Found in signature block
  0.4 = Inferred from context
  0.1 = Not found (using placeholder)

status_confidence: How confident AI is status is correct
  1.0 = Clear explicit signals ("We regret to inform you" = rejected)
  0.7 = Strong signals (multiple keywords)
  0.4 = Moderate signals (some keywords, ambiguous tone)
  0.1 = Unclear (could be anything)
